// 01 · about

Passionate About Building Solutions For Students.

I'm Imran, a Computer Science student at Sukkur IBA University and the founder of EduCareerInfo.

I'm from Larkana, Sindh, and I'm studying BS Computer Science on the STHP Fully Funded Scholarship, with graduation expected in 2028. Before computer science I completed an Associate Degree in Education and taught in real classrooms, which shaped how I explain things and why I care about good guidance.

In 2023 I started EduCareerInfo, a student-focused platform in Pakistan for mock tests, preparation material, admission guidance and career counselling. I build and maintain the tools behind it myself, in PHP and MySQL.

Today I'm sharpening my programming fundamentals, building projects, and learning cybersecurity as a CTF player on TryHackMe, always in authorised labs and with a defensive mindset.

What I'm working toward

  • Becoming a capable computer scientist with strong fundamentals in programming, databases and security.
  • Growing EduCareerInfo into a dependable home for student resources, now moving to educareerinfo.tech.
  • Building useful, well-crafted technology that makes opportunities easier to reach.
